About Me

Creating a Better World at Work.

Bridging the gap between human behavior and organizational excellence through psychological insight and financial rigor.

I've always been curious about people—what drives them, how they make decisions, and the way they connect with each other. That curiosity led me to study Psychology, where I discovered just how much human behavior shapes both personal growth and workplace success.

During my internships, I worked with people from all walks of life — childs, teens, and adults — and saw how early experiences influence future behavior. It showed me the power of empathy, patience, and thoughtful support. That's when it clicked: I wanted to create environments where people feel understood, valued, and able to thrive.

My journey began not with a career plan, but with a question about why we connect... and this realization drew me to Human Resources. It felt like the perfect space to bring everything I'd learned to life—supporting people, fostering growth, and helping organizations build stronger, more human workplaces.

What excites me most about HR is the chance to make a real impact on people's lives. I'm driven by the belief that when people feel seen and supported, they do their best work. For me, HR isn't just a career; it's a way to make work more human.

With a background in public speaking and debate, I bring clarity, active listening, and persuasive communication to complex employee relations, conflict resolution, and leadership strategy. I've learned how to connect with different perspectives and have thoughtful, honest conversations.

Behavioral Talent Mapping

Every hire is a strategic move — I ensure the right people are in the right seats at the right time by applying psychological principles and objective scorecards to decode candidate potential, streamline recruitment velocity, ensure long-term cultural alignment, reduce time-to-hire via Boolean sourcing, behavioral screenings and structured evaluation frameworks.

Employee Autonomy

When employees own their growth, the entire company rises with them. I'm empowering workforce autonomy and driving retention by engineering transparent policy frameworks, equitable performance pathways, and proactive dispute resolution mechanisms that transform organizational friction into scalable success.

Operational & Fiscal Goals

I'm bridging human capital strategy with fiscal discipline by combining precision payroll operations and statutory compliance with active OpEx auditing to optimize budgets and maximize bottom-line efficiency.
